Grade: 6th – 8th
Duration: 45 min
Topic: Rights, Responsibilities, Privacy

📖 Lesson Content

What Is Digital Citizenship?

Definition: Being a responsible, ethical, respectful participant in the digital world.

It includes:

  • Digital Rights: You have the right to privacy, safety, and respectful treatment online
  • Digital Responsibilities: You're responsible for treating others with respect, protecting privacy, following laws
  • Digital Access: Not everyone has equal access to technology. We need to be aware of this.
  • Digital Literacy: Understanding how to use tech responsibly and critically

Key Concept: Consent

Consent means: Getting permission before sharing someone's photo, information, or words online.

Examples:

  • ❌ Wrong: Posting a friend's photo without asking
  • ✅ Right: "Can I post this photo of you?"
  • ❌ Wrong: Sharing someone's personal info they told you in confidence
  • ✅ Right: "Is it OK if I tell people about this?"

Scenario Discussion

Discuss as a class:

Scenario 1: A friend posted an embarrassing photo of you. Your options: (a) Demand they take it down, (b) Report it, (c) Post a mean photo of them back, (d) Talk to them calmly first.
What's most responsible?

Scenario 2: You see someone spreading a rumor about a classmate online. Do you: (a) Retweet/share, (b) Ignore it, (c) Tell them to stop and report it?
Which shows good citizenship?

Scenario 3: A stranger online wants to be your friend. You: (a) Accept right away, (b) Ask your parents first, (c) Investigate who they are first?
What's safer?

Your Digital Citizenship Pledge

Have students write their own pledge. Examples:

"I promise to: ask before sharing photos of friends, treat others with kindness online, protect my passwords, and report cyberbullying when I see it."

📧 Parent Guide
Digital Citizenship at Home & School

What We're Teaching

Digital citizenship means being a responsible, respectful participant online—just like being a good citizen in your community.

Key Concepts

Rights: Privacy, safety, respect

Responsibilities: Treat others kindly, ask for consent, protect personal info, follow rules

Discussion Starters

  • "What does it mean to be a good digital citizen to you?"
  • "Do you ask permission before posting photos of friends?"
  • "How would you want someone to treat you online?"

Emergency Support & Crisis Hotlines

Immediate help when someone needs it most.

In immediate danger? Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number. If you suspect a child is being sexually exploited online, report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) CyberTipline.

Crisis Text Line

For emotional distress, crisis, or bullying support

Text: HOME to 741741

Available 24/7, free and confidential

Can help with: Emotional distress, bullying, depression, anxiety, self-harm thoughts

NCMEC CyberTipline

For reporting suspected child sexual exploitation online

Online Report: CyberTipline.org

Or Call: 1-800-843-5678

Available 24/7; coordinates with law enforcement on eligible reports

Go to CyberTipline

Official scope: Online child sexual exploitation and abuse material

24/7, free and confidential

For sexual assault or abuse support

Call: 1-800-656-4673 (HOPE)

Or Text: HOPE to 64673

24/7, confidential

Provides: Confidential support for sexual assault and abuse survivors

When to Call vs. Text vs. Report Online

Call When:

  • Someone is in immediate danger
  • Someone is having thoughts of suicide
  • You suspect abuse or exploitation happening now
  • You need to report a crime

Text When:

  • Someone feels safer texting (more private)
  • It's during school/work and calling isn't possible
  • Someone is struggling emotionally
  • You need to talk but can't make a voice call

Report Online When:

  • You have screenshots of exploitation
  • You want to document predatory messages
  • You need a formal report for law enforcement
  • You prefer detailed written documentation

Important to Know

  • Cost, privacy, and confidentiality policies vary by service and circumstance. Check the service's official information if you have questions about what applies to your situation.
  • Text lines are staffed by real people, not bots. They're trained in crisis support and genuinely care.
  • You don't need to have proof or be sure of the details to call or text. If you're concerned, that's reason enough to reach out.
  • If you're not sure what to do next, visit Something Happened? for situation-specific guidance on responding to online incidents.
  • CyberTipline reports: The NCMEC CyberTipline accepts reports of suspected online child sexual exploitation and coordinates with law enforcement.

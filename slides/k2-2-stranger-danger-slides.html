🚨 Stranger Danger

Who Can You Trust Online?

Not everyone online is who they say they are!

Someone might say they're a kid...

But they could be anyone! 😟

⚠️ Red Flags

  • Asking your name or where you live
  • Asking your school or what school
  • Wants to be your friend but you don't know them
  • Asking for personal information

Who CAN You Trust?

Trusted adults in YOUR LIFE:

  • 👨‍👩‍👧 Mom or Dad
  • 👵👴 Grandparents
  • 👨‍🏫 Teachers
  • 😊 School counselor

Safe or NOT Safe?

A kid from your school asks to play a game online

✅ SAFE!

(You know them in real life)

Safe or NOT Safe?

Someone online says "Hi! I'm 8 like you. What's your name?"

❌ NOT SAFE!

(Tell a grown-up!)

What To Do:

🛑 DON'T give personal info

🛑 DON'T click bad links

✅ TELL A GROWN-UP

You're SAFE!

Because you know what to do!

Tell a trusted adult. Always. 💪

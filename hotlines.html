🆘 Emergency Support & Crisis Hotlines

Immediate help when your family needs it most.

⚠️ In immediate danger? Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number. If someone is threatening your child online, report to the National Center for Missing & Exploited Children (NCMEC) immediately.

When to Call vs. Text vs. Report Online

📞 Call When:

  • Someone is in immediate danger
  • Your child is having thoughts of suicide
  • You suspect abuse or exploitation happening now
  • You need to report a crime

💬 Text When:

  • Your child feels safer texting (more private)
  • It's during school/work and calling isn't possible
  • Your child is struggling emotionally
  • You need to talk but can't make a voice call

🌐 Report Online When:

  • You have screenshots of exploitation
  • You want to document predatory messages
  • You need a formal report for law enforcement
  • You prefer detailed written documentation

📌 Important to Know

  • All of these services are free and confidential. They won't judge your family or get you in trouble.
  • Text lines are staffed by real people, not bots. They're trained in crisis support and genuinely care.
  • Reports to NCMEC CyberTipline go to law enforcement. Law enforcement takes digital crimes very seriously.
  • You don't need to have proof to call. If you're concerned, that's enough reason to reach out.
  • If your child won't talk to you, these services can help them process what happened in a safe space.
  • Screenshot everything. Before deleting messages or blocking someone, take screenshots as evidence.

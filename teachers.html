📚 How to Teach These Lessons

Lesson Structure

  • Opening (5-10 min): Hook with a real scenario or question to get students thinking
  • Teach (15-20 min): Present key concepts, real examples, and statistics
  • Discuss (10-15 min): Use discussion prompts to engage students in thinking critically
  • Activity (10-15 min): Interactive activity—debate, case study, role-play, or small group discussion
  • Wrap-up (5 min): Key takeaways and why this matters to their lives

🔧 Technical Setup

Projector for slides, students can work in groups or individually depending on activity. Internet access needed for some discussion topics (showing real examples safely).

💬 Handling Difficult Questions

If a student discloses abuse: Stay calm, thank them for telling you, don't promise to keep it secret. Report to school counselor/admin and follow mandatory reporting requirements. If students get upset: Normalize it—these are heavy topics. Validate feelings. Offer to connect them with school counselor.

⚠️ Mandatory Reporting

These lessons may trigger disclosures of abuse, self-harm, or exploitation. Know your state's mandatory reporting requirements and your school's procedures. Have counselor contact info ready.

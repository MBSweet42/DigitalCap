🔐 Privacy Settings Setup Guide
Step-by-step for Instagram, TikTok, Snapchat, Discord, and more. Make these changes TODAY.
WHY THIS MATTERS

Default settings on social apps are designed to maximize engagement and data collection—not protect your child's privacy. Changing these settings can:

  • Limit who can contact your child
  • Prevent strangers from finding their location
  • Reduce algorithm-driven addiction
  • Protect their personal information
INSTAGRAM PRIVACY SETUP
Essential Settings:
  • Settings → Privacy → Account Private (Make it private—strangers can't see their posts)
  • Settings → Privacy → Who can see your story? → Close Friends only (not public)
  • Settings → Privacy → Who can send you messages? → Friends only (not Everyone)
  • Settings → Privacy → Allow message requests from non-followers? → OFF
  • Settings → Location → Turn off location access entirely
  • Settings → Activity Status → OFF (so no one knows when they're online)
Why these matter:
A private account means strangers (and predators) can't follow them without approval. Limiting DMs prevents creepy messages. Hiding activity status prevents being tracked.
TIKTOK PRIVACY SETUP
Essential Settings:
  • Settings → Privacy and safety → Account privacy → Private account
  • Settings → Privacy and safety → Who can message me? → Friends only
  • Settings → Privacy and safety → Who can comment? → Friends only
  • Settings → Privacy and safety → Who can see your watch history? → Nobody
  • Settings → Notifications → Disable notifications (stops algorithm addiction)
  • Settings → Wellbeing → Restrict logging in to recommended times only
Why these matter:
TikTok's algorithm is designed to be addictive. Turning off notifications reduces urges to check. Restricting message senders prevents contact from strangers. Limiting watch history prevents algorithm tracking.
SNAPCHAT PRIVACY SETUP
Essential Settings:
  • Settings → Who can contact me? → Friends (not Everyone)
  • Settings → Who can see my location? → Nobody (turn off Snap Map)
  • Settings → Who can view my story? → Friends only
  • Settings → Notifications → Turn off
  • Settings → Location Services → Disable (in phone settings, not just app)
Why these matter:
Snapchat's Snap Map lets anyone see exactly where your child is in real-time. This is a major safety risk. Allowing friends-only contact prevents strangers from reaching them.
DISCORD PRIVACY SETUP
Essential Settings:
  • User Settings → Privacy & Safety → Restrict who can DM you to friends only
  • User Settings → Privacy & Safety → Turn off Allow direct messages from server members
  • User Settings → Privacy & Safety → Explicit content filter → ON
  • User Settings → Voice & Video → Voice activity should be ON (not automatic push-to-talk with strangers)
  • Review Servers they've joined—Discord servers can have predators. Only allow joining pre-vetted/school servers
Why these matter:
Discord is a favorite platform for gamers but has predator risks. Limiting DMs prevents grooming. Explicit content filter blocks adult servers. Ask to approve servers before joining.
FACEBOOK & MESSENGER PRIVACY SETUP
Essential Settings:
  • Settings → Privacy → Who can see your posts? → Friends only
  • Settings → Privacy → Who can message you? → Friends only
  • Settings → Apps and Websites → Review connected apps (remove any they don't recognize)
  • Messenger → People → Who can contact you? → Friends only
  • Location Services (in phone settings) → OFF for Facebook
PHONE-LEVEL PRIVACY SETTINGS
For iPhone (iOS):
  • Settings → Privacy → Location Services → OFF (or per-app: turn off for social apps)
  • Settings → Privacy → Microphone → Review and deny access to unnecessary apps
  • Settings → Privacy → Camera → Review and deny access to unnecessary apps
  • Settings → App Store → Require password for purchases (or use Screen Time parental controls)
  • Settings → Screen Time → Enable, set app limits, and block at app store (requires parent password)
For Android:
  • Settings → Apps & notifications → Permissions → Location → Review and deny
  • Settings → Apps & notifications → Permissions → Camera → Review and deny
  • Settings → Apps & notifications → Permissions → Microphone → Review and deny
  • Settings → Google Play Store → Parental controls → Set PIN and restrict content rating
  • Settings → Digital Wellbeing → Enable app timers and downtime
💡 IMPORTANT REMINDERS
  • Do this WITH your child. Explain why each setting matters. They're more likely to keep settings if they understand.
  • These settings may change. Apps update their interface regularly. Review settings every 3-6 months.
  • Settings + conversations = safety. Privacy settings alone don't prevent problems. Have ongoing conversations about online safety too.
  • Check what apps they actually have. These are the most dangerous, but new apps appear constantly. Ask them what's popular now.

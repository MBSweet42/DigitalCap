Read each situation. Write YES if it's cyberbullying, NO if it's not:
1. Someone posts a mean comment on your photo saying "You're ugly"
Cyberbullying? _____
2. A friend texts you to say they disagree with something you said
Cyberbullying? _____
3. Someone spreads a rumor about you on social media that isn't true
Cyberbullying? _____
4. Someone says "Nice drawing!" on your post
Cyberbullying? _____
5. A group of people repeatedly send mean messages to your friend
Cyberbullying? _____
🦸 Activity 2: Be an Upstander!
If you see cyberbullying, what can YOU do? Check (✓) the RIGHT choices:
☐ Laugh at the mean comments
☐ Post a kind comment on their post
☐ Message them privately to say you support them
☐ Share the mean comments with others
☐ Tell a teacher or adult
☐ Report the mean posts to the app
💭 Activity 3: What Would You Do?
Write what you would do in these situations:
Situation 1: Someone is being bullied online. What do you do?
Situation 2: You're being cyberbullied. What's your first step?

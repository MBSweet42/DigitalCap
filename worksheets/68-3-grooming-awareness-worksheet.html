Activity 1: Recognizing Manipulation Tactics
Grooming happens when someone builds trust to manipulate you. Check off the RED FLAGS you recognize:
Suspicious behavior patterns:
☐ Asks personal questions (name, age, school, where you live, when parents aren't home)
☐ Gives lots of compliments or says "you're so mature for your age"
☐ Says "your parents don't understand you, but I do"
☐ Asks to keep the relationship or conversations secret
☐ Sends unexpected gifts, money, or game credits
☐ Asks for photos or videos of you
☐ Wants to move conversations to private apps or messaging
☐ Tries to isolate you from friends and family
☐ Asks to meet in person or get your phone number
☐ Pressures you or makes you feel guilty for not responding quickly
How many red flags did you recognize? All of them are warning signs. Trust your gut feeling.

Activity 3: Safety Response Plan
If someone online makes you uncomfortable, here's what to do. Fill in your action plan:
Step 1: STOP responding immediately
Do NOT continue the conversation or meet them in person.
Step 2: SAVE evidence (if safe to do so)
Screenshot concerning messages so you have proof.
Step 3: BLOCK and REPORT the person
Block them on that platform and report them for suspicious behavior.
Step 4: TELL A TRUSTED ADULT
This could be: parent, guardian, school counselor, teacher, or coach.

💚 Online Kindness

Standing Up to Cyberbullying

What's Cyberbullying?

Mean messages online that hurt someone's feelings

Making fun of someone publicly

Spreading rumors about someone online

Why Cyberbullying Hurts

  • 😢 It's permanent (screenshots)
  • 👥 Everyone can see it
  • 💔 It feels very lonely
  • 🌙 It follows you home

The Bystander Effect

When mean things happen and people do NOTHING...

The bullies feel more powerful 😢

Be an UPSTANDER!

💬 Don't like, laugh, or comment on mean posts

😊 Make kind comments instead

👨‍🤝‍👨 Be friends with people being bullied

🚨 Tell a grown-up

If You're Being Bullied

  • ✋ Don't respond to mean messages
  • 📱 Screenshot it as evidence
  • 🛑 Block the person
  • 🗣️ Tell a trusted adult

Real Talk: You're Not Alone

Many kids experience cyberbullying

It's NOT your fault

Adults can help you

Choose Kindness! 💚

The world needs YOUR kindness

Be an upstander! 🌟

📱 App Safety Checklist
Before downloading an app, use this checklist to evaluate the real risks
🔍 BASIC INFORMATION
What is this app designed to do?
Understand the app's primary purpose before allowing access
Is there a minimum age requirement?
Check official app stores (Apple/Google) for age ratings
Who developed this app?
Verify it's from a legitimate, established company or creator
🔐 PRIVACY & DATA
Does it ask for permission to access location?
Question why the app needs to know where your child is
Does it ask for camera/microphone access?
Only grant if essential to the app's function
Does it require a login or account?
If yes, review privacy policy and parental controls first
Is the privacy policy easy to understand?
Red flag if it's written in confusing legal language
💬 COMMUNICATION & SOCIAL
Can your child chat with strangers?
Look for unsupervised messaging, live chat, or multiplayer features
Are there reporting/blocking tools?
Can your child easily report harassment or inappropriate behavior?
💰 IN-APP SPENDING
Does the app offer in-app purchases?
Enable purchase restrictions in your device settings
Does it have a battle pass or premium currency?
These are designed to encourage spending
💡 PARENT TIP
Keep this checklist handy and review it BEFORE saying yes to a new app. Have a conversation with your child about why you're asking these questions—it teaches them to think critically about privacy and safety too.

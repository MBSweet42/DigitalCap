🤝 Digital Kindness & Screen Manners

Being kind online is just like being kind in person!

What is the internet?

  • 📱 A place where we connect with friends
  • 🎮 A place to play games
  • 📺 A place to watch videos
  • 💬 A place to talk to people

The SAME rules of kindness apply!

Screen Manners Rule #1

Use Kind Words!

  • Say "please" ✓
  • Say "thank you" ✓
  • Say "great idea!" ✓
  • Don't say mean words ✗

Screen Manners Rule #2

Take Turns!

  • Listen when others talk
  • Wait your turn
  • Don't interrupt

Screen Manners Rule #3

Include Others!

If someone wants to join your game...

Say YES! 🎉

What if someone is mean?

  • ❌ Don't be mean back
  • ✅ Tell a grown-up
  • ✅ Block them if you can
  • ✅ It's not your fault

Ask Before Sharing!

Before you share a picture of your friend...

ASK FIRST!

"Is it OK if I share this picture of you?"

You're Doing GREAT! 🌟

Remember:

Be KIND online, just like you are offline!

🤖 AI, Deepfakes & Misinformation

The truth in a world of fakes

What's AI?

Artificial Intelligence = computer systems trained to perform tasks

  • 📝 Write text
  • 🎨 Generate images
  • 🎬 Create videos
  • 🔍 Predict behavior

What's a Deepfake?

AI-created fake videos/audio that look REAL

A deepfake could show someone saying things they never said

Or doing things they never did 🎬

Deepfake Dangers

  • 😱 Blackmail & extortion
  • 🗳️ Election manipulation
  • 💔 Harassment & humiliation
  • 🤥 Spreading disinformation
  • 📱 Non-consensual intimate content

What's Misinformation?

FALSE information spread as if it were true

  • ❌ Fake news stories
  • ❌ Misleading headlines
  • ❌ Fake science claims
  • ❌ Conspiracy theories

The SIFT Method

How to verify information:

  • Stop - Don't share immediately
  • Investigate - Check the source
  • Find better coverage - Look for multiple sources
  • Trace claims - Where did this come from?

Red Flags for Misinformation

  • 😠 Extreme emotions (outrage, fear, excitement)
  • 📰 No credible sources cited
  • ⚠️ Sensational headlines
  • 🔗 Sketchy website design
  • 👤 Anonymous author with no credentials

Question Everything 🤔

Don't assume something is true just because you saw it online

Be a critical thinker! 🧠

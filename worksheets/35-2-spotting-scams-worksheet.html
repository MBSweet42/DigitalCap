📋 Activity 2: Scam Detective Checklist
Use this checklist to identify scams:
☐ Does it say "Act NOW!" or "URGENT"?
☐ Is it too good to be true?
☐ Does it ask for passwords or personal info?
☐ Does the website URL look weird?
☐ Are there spelling mistakes?
If you check 2 or more... IT'S A SCAM!

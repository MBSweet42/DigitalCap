Activity 2: The SIFT Method - Fact-Checking Practice
Use the SIFT method (Stop, Investigate, Find better coverage, Trace) to evaluate a claim:
1. STOP — Pause before sharing
Ask yourself: Is this real? Do I actually know this is true, or just feel like it is?
2. INVESTIGATE the SOURCE — Who published this?
Is it from a credible news outlet? A random account? What's their bias or motivation?
3. FIND BETTER COVERAGE — Search for corroboration
Do other credible sources report the same thing? Or is this only one outlet?
4. TRACE THE CLAIM — Where did it originate?
Is it taken out of context? Does the original source match what the post claims?

About the Digital Safety Hub

Our Mission

The Digital Safety Hub exists to help families navigate the digital world safely, respectfully, and confidently. Whether you're a parent trying to understand your child's apps, a young person learning to protect yourself online, or a family looking to build healthy digital habits, this is a resource built for you.

How We Build Our Content

All information on the Digital Safety Hub is adapted from trusted, authoritative sources in digital safety, child development, and online security. We don't write original research—we synthesize and simplify information from the experts so it's easy to understand and use.

Our content is reviewed regularly to stay current with evolving apps, trends, and threats.

Sources & Credits

We adapt content from:

  • Common Sense Media — Independent nonprofit providing media reviews, parental guidance, and digital literacy resources
  • NCMEC/NetSmartz — National Center for Missing & Exploited Children's awareness and prevention programs
  • FTC OnGuardOnline — Federal Trade Commission's consumer education site for internet safety and privacy
  • ConnectSafely — Nonprofit organization providing research-based digital safety education
  • Internet Matters — UK-based charity helping families navigate digital life safely
  • StaySafeOnline.org — National Cyber Security Alliance's awareness and education programs
  • TikTok, YouTube, Instagram, Discord, and other platforms' official safety centers

Privacy & Data

The Digital Safety Hub is designed to work entirely in your browser. We don't collect personal information, track your activity, or store your data. Your quiz scores and checklist progress are saved locally on your device only.

If you use this site on multiple devices, your progress won't sync between them—that's by design, to protect your privacy.

Not Medical or Legal Advice

The Digital Safety Hub is an educational resource, not a substitute for professional advice. If your child is experiencing severe cyberbullying, self-harm, or abuse, please contact:

  • CyberTipline: cybertipline.org (report exploitation to NCMEC)
  • Crisis Text Line: Text HOME to 741741
  • National Suicide Prevention Lifeline: 988
  • Local law enforcement or emergency services if you're in immediate danger

Is Something Wrong?

Found inaccurate information, a broken link, or an app we should add? This resource is meant to help families, so your feedback matters.

Please reach out with corrections or suggestions. Your input helps us improve the hub for everyone.

License & Use

The Digital Safety Hub is designed to be useful and shareable. Feel free to:

  • Share the site with other parents and educators
  • Print pages for offline use or classroom discussions
  • Use it as a reference in presentations or guides

If you're using it in a professional or educational context, please attribute the original sources we cite (Common Sense Media, FTC, NCMEC, etc.).

Acknowledgments

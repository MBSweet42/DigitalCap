🆘 Emergency Hotlines & Crisis Support

Immediate help when your family needs it most. All services are FREE, confidential, and available 24/7.

💬 Crisis Text Line

For mental health crisis, bullying, emotional distress

Text: HOME to 741741

🚨 NCMEC CyberTipline

For exploitation, predators, grooming, sextortion

📱 1-800-843-5678 | 🌐 CyberTipline.org

💙 Suicide Prevention

For suicide risk or self-harm thoughts

Call or Text: 988

🛡️ Cyberbullying Hotline

For online harassment and cyberbullying

Call: 1-800-420-1479

⚠️ In immediate danger? Call 911 (US) or your local emergency number.

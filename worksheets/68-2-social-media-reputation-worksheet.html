Activity 1: Understanding the Algorithm
The algorithm learns what you like and shows you more. Reflect on your own social media experience:
1. Think of a social media app you use. What kinds of posts or videos do you see most?
2. What behaviors teach the algorithm what you like? (Examples: likes, comments, watch time, shares)
3. Do you think the algorithm is designed to help you OR keep you scrolling as long as possible?
4. How can you be more intentional about what feeds you see and how long you spend scrolling?
Activity 2: Digital Permanence & Future Consequences
Posts can be deleted, but nothing ever truly disappears from the internet. Analyze these scenarios:
Scenario A: You post a silly, sarcastic comment about your school. It gets 50 likes and 20 comments. You laugh and move on. One year later, you're applying to high school programs and the program director searches your name online... and finds that comment.

Scenario B: Your friend convinces you to do a goofy TikTok dance. You're not sure about it, but you do it anyway. Later, you regret it, delete the video, and think it's gone. Five years later at age 18, someone finds a screenshot and shares it.

The gut check before posting: "Would I want my parents, teacher, or future college/employer seeing this?"

🛑 Activity 2: What Would You Do?
For each concerning situation, circle the RIGHT answer:
1. Someone online asks where you go to school.
Circle: ☐ Tell them    ☐ DON'T tell them / TELL A GROWN-UP
2. Someone online says: "Let's keep this our secret."
Circle: ☐ Keep the secret    ☐ DON'T keep secrets / TELL A GROWN-UP
3. Someone online is giving you lots of compliments and says they're your best friend.
Circle: ☐ Be happy about it    ☐ Feel something is wrong / TELL A GROWN-UP

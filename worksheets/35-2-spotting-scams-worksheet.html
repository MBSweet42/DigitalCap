🚨 Activity 1: Red Flag Check
Read each sentence. Circle ALL the RED FLAGS (warning signs of a scam):
Message 1:
"Congratulations! You won a free iPhone! Click here NOW to claim it! Expires today!"

Red flags: ☐ Too good to be true   ☐ Urgency ("NOW" / "today")   ☐ Asks you to click
Message 2:
"Hi! Your library book is due tomorrow. Return it to avoid a late fee."

Red flags: ☐ Too good to be true   ☐ Urgency   ☐ Asks for password/money
Message 3:
"VERIFY YOUR AMAZON ACCOUNT! Click this link: amazzon-secure.net. Enter your password NOW!"

Red flags: ☐ Spelling mistake (amazzon vs amazon)   ☐ Asks for password   ☐ Fake link
Message 4:
"You've been selected to receive $50 Amazon gift card! No strings attached!"

Red flags: ☐ Too good to be true   ☐ Asking for payment   ☐ Suspicious sender

🛑 Activity 3: What Would You Do?
If you got a message that looks like a scam, circle the RIGHT answer(s):
1. Should you click on the suspicious link?
☐ Yes (to check if it's real)     ☐ No (it's safer to not click)
2. Should you respond to the message?
☐ Yes (ask them questions)     ☐ No (don't engage with scammers)
3. What should you do?
☐ Tell a trusted adult     ☐ Block the sender     ☐ Report it to the platform
✅ THE THREE RULES:
1. DON'T CLICK suspicious links
2. DON'T RESPOND to scam messages
3. DO TELL a trusted adult right away!

How DigitalCap Approaches AI

AI is not automatically dangerous. AI is not automatically trustworthy. AI can be useful. AI can make mistakes. Understanding how it works gives people more control over how they use it.

Our role is to explain what the technology does, what it can be useful for, what limitations or risks exist, what choices people have, what actions they can take, and where information came from.

This section is for anyone—adults, teens, families, caregivers, older adults, educators, and anyone trying to navigate AI in daily life.

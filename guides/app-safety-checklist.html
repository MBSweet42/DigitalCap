How to Evaluate an App

Find information about any app, understand what it can do, and decide what makes sense for you.

Start by checking DigitalCap App Check if the app you're researching may already be reviewed. App Check provides capabilities, safeguards, age guidance, and sourced safety information.

If the app is not reviewed yet, or if you want to investigate further yourself, this guide helps you work through the important areas. A capability is not automatically a problem. The goal is to understand what the app can do, what access it provides, what controls exist, and whether that fits your needs.

You can work through this checklist on screen, or print a blank copy and fill it out by hand.

Confirm you have the right app

Before investigating, make sure you're researching the correct app. Apps can have similar names. Copycat or outdated listings exist. Researching the wrong version will produce misleading information.

Checklist

Understand what the app does

A capability is not automatically a problem. The goal is to understand what is possible, then decide if that fits your needs.

Consider

Check permissions

Apps request permission to access your device's features. Understanding why an app needs each permission helps you make an informed choice.

Key question: Does this permission make sense for the feature I want to use?

Common permissions

Most device settings let you review or change permissions later. You don't necessarily have to say yes to every permission to use an app.

Privacy & data

Find and review what information the app collects and how it's used. Privacy policies can be difficult to read. Start with official sources (app-store listing, developer website, official help pages) rather than third-party summaries.

Look for

Communication & other users

If an app connects people, understand how contact and communication work. Communicating with people you don't already know is not automatically harmful. Exposure depends on who can start contact, available privacy controls, whether reporting/blocking tools exist, moderation practices, and how the app is being used.

Consider

Content & external access

Understand how content reaches you and where it can lead. An app may appear to have a limited purpose while still providing access to broader content through links, browsers, videos, or chat. Understanding these pathways helps you make an informed choice.

Investigate

Purchases & subscriptions

Understand potential costs and advertising before downloading. The official app-store listing usually shows pricing and purchase options. Review before downloading to understand potential costs.

Check

Safeguards & controls

Investigate what tools exist to manage your experience and protect yourself. Safeguards can change how exposure works, but no setting guarantees perfect protection. Investigate what tools exist to help you maintain control.

Look for

Check multiple sources

Compare information from multiple credible sources before deciding. User reviews can reveal patterns and real experiences. A single viral post, TikTok, or Reddit comment should not automatically be treated as proof. Look for consensus across multiple sources instead of relying on one story.

Does this app fit what you need?

Use this framework to organize what you've learned. Remember: Two people can reasonably make different choices about the same app. What works for one person may not work for another.

What can this app do?

What features, access, and connections does it enable?

What could that expose me to?

What information is collected? Who might contact me? What content could I encounter? Can it send me outside the app?

What controls exist?

What privacy settings, blocking tools, content filters, or parental controls are available?

What matters in my situation?

My age, needs, maturity, how I plan to use it, who I know on it, my experience with similar apps.

Does this make sense for me?

Given everything above, what choice feels right?

Next steps

Check if DigitalCap has reviewed this app for more detailed information about capabilities and safeguards.

Review privacy settings on your device or this specific app to adjust what permissions and access you allow.

Have a conversation with someone you trust about your choice—a parent, caregiver, teacher, counselor, or friend.

Take protective steps to stay safe online, manage device time, and handle situations that come up.

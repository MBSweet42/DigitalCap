🛡️ Recognizing Concerning Behavior Online

Staying safe and trusting your feelings

What Does Concerning Mean?

It means something feels uncomfortable or wrong

You can feel it in your body and your feelings

And that's IMPORTANT! 👂

⚠️ Red Flag: Asking Personal Questions

"What's your name?"

"Where do you live?"

"What school do you go to?"

❌ These are RED FLAGS!

⚠️ Red Flag: Asking for Secrets

"Don't tell your mom and dad"

"This can be our secret"

❌ NEVER!

Real friends don't ask for secrets!

⚠️ Red Flag: Too Friendly Too Fast

Lots of compliments right away:

"You're so special!"

"You're my best friend!"

"Nobody understands you like I do"

Real friendships take time! ⏰

⚠️ Red Flag: Asking for Pictures

"Send me a picture"

Even if they say they're your age...

❌ NO!

Never share pictures with people online!

Trust Your GUT! 🎯

If something feels wrong...

It probably IS wrong!

Your feelings are SMART

Tell a trusted grown-up RIGHT AWAY

You're SAFE! ✅

It's NEVER your fault if someone acts wrong

Tell an adult. Always. 💪

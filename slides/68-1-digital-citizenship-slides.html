🌐 Digital Citizenship Basics

Being a good citizen online

What is Digital Citizenship?

Being RESPECTFUL, RESPONSIBLE, and SAFE online

Just like being a good citizen in your community

Your Digital Rights

  • 🔐 Right to privacy
  • 💬 Right to speak freely (respectfully)
  • 🛡️ Right to be safe from bullying
  • ⚖️ Right to fair treatment

Your Digital Responsibilities

  • ✅ Respect others' privacy
  • ✅ Ask before sharing others' photos
  • ✅ Don't spread rumors or lies
  • ✅ Treat people with kindness

Understanding Consent

Before you share someone's:

  • 📱 Photo
  • 💭 Private conversation
  • 📍 Location
  • 🎮 Gaming username

ASK FIRST!

Privacy in the Digital World

🔒 Use strong passwords

🔒 Don't share personal details with strangers

🔒 Know your privacy settings

🔒 Be careful what you post

Good Digital Citizens:

  • 🤝 Are kind and respectful
  • 🛡️ Stand up against bullying
  • 🔐 Protect their own privacy
  • 💭 Think before they post
  • 🗣️ Tell adults about problems

You ARE a Digital Citizen! 🌐

Own your responsibility

Make the online world better! 🌟

📱 Social Media & Your Digital Reputation

What you share shapes who you are online

The Algorithm Knows You

Platforms track what you:

  • 👀 Like and watch
  • ⏱️ Spend time on
  • 👍 Engage with
  • 🔍 Search for

They use this to show you more similar content

Echo Chambers

If you only see one viewpoint...

You might think everyone agrees with you

But the world is WAY more diverse! 🌈

⚠️ Mental Health Impacts

  • 😔 Comparing yourself to others
  • ⏰ Spending too much time scrolling
  • 😰 FOMO (Fear Of Missing Out)
  • 😢 Anxiety from seeking likes/comments

Permanence & Screenshots

Even stories and snaps aren't REALLY private

Someone can screenshot or record

Then it lives FOREVER 📸

Building a Positive Reputation

✅ Post things you're proud of

✅ Be genuine, not fake

✅ Show kindness online

✅ Take breaks from scrolling

Healthy Social Media Use

  • ⏱️ Set time limits
  • 🔕 Turn off notifications
  • 🚫 Unfollow accounts that make you feel bad
  • 🤝 Connect with real friends
  • 📚 Do things offline

You Control Your Reputation 📱

Think. Share. Be Proud.

Your online presence matters! 🌟

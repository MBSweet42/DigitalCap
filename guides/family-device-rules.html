📋 Family Device Rules
Printable house rules for phones, tablets, and computers. Post on the fridge or child's door.
THE RULES
Rule 1
No phones at meals or family time
Family dinners, car rides, and activities are device-free. This includes parents—model the behavior you want.
If broken: Device is confiscated for the rest of the day.
Rule 2
No screens one hour before bed
Blue light keeps your brain awake. Put devices in the charging station by 8 PM (or 1 hour before your bedtime).
If broken: Device is taken until the next morning.
Rule 3
Always ask before downloading a new app
We'll check it together using the DigitalCap App Safety Checklist. No exceptions—this protects your privacy and safety.
If broken: App is deleted and screen time is reduced for one week.
Rule 4
Never share personal information online
Real name, phone number, address, school name, or location (via check-ins) stay private. Even if a "friend" asks.
If broken: Loss of device access for one week + conversation about why this matters.
Rule 5
Tell an adult immediately if something feels weird or unsafe
Mean comments, strange messages from adults, pressure from friends, or anything that makes you uncomfortable. NO punishment will happen—we're here to help.
Reward: We work together to solve the problem. Your safety is more important than the rule.
Rule 6
Respect others' privacy and property online
Don't share other people's photos, videos, or personal info. Don't cyberbully or spread rumors. The internet is permanent.
If broken: Device access suspended until the person harmed receives an apology. Screen time reduced for two weeks.
Rule 7
Ask before making purchases inside apps
In-app spending adds up fast. Even small purchases require permission. If the rule is broken, you pay the charge from your allowance.
If broken: You reimburse the cost. Device confiscated for one week.
Rule 8
Parents have access to all accounts and passwords
We're not spying—we're keeping you safe. We may check your device occasionally, and you should be okay with that.
Note: Privacy is earned through trust. Follow these rules, and we'll respect your growing independence.

💡 TIPS FOR SUCCESS
  • Make the rules together, not alone. Kids are more likely to follow rules they helped create.
  • Post these rules somewhere visible—kitchen, bedroom, device itself.
  • Revisit every 6 months. Rules may need to change as kids get older.
  • Be consistent. Enforce rules equally for all children. No exceptions "just this once."
  • Be kind. The goal is safety, not punishment. Make adjustments if rules aren't working.

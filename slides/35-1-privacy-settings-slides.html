Privacy & Safety Settings

Why Privacy Settings Matter

Apps like Instagram, TikTok, and Snapchat collect information about you

You need to control what they can see!

Key Privacy Risks

  • ❌ Public posts seen by anyone
  • ❌ Location being shared
  • ❌ Strangers sending direct messages
  • ❌ Data being collected for ads

Instagram Settings You Should Check

  • 🔒 Make your account PRIVATE
  • 👤 Only approved followers see posts
  • 💬 Control who messages you
  • 📍 Turn OFF location tags

TikTok & Snapchat Settings

  • 🔒 Set account to PRIVATE
  • 👁️ Limit who can see your videos
  • 💬 Turn off comments from strangers
  • 🎮 Don't use location features

Discord Safety Tips

  • 🔐 Use a STRONG password
  • 🚫 Don't share personal info
  • ❌ Block/report mean people
  • 📢 Be careful in public servers

Your Action Plan

1️⃣ Open one app right now

2️⃣ Find the Settings menu

3️⃣ Make your account PRIVATE

4️⃣ Turn off location sharing

You're in Control! 🛡️

Adjust settings often - apps change all the time
